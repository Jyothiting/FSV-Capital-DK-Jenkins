**Question:** What traction do idea-stage startups need to apply for funding?

**UI:** Search → **Ask AI (RAG)** → badge **Retrieval excerpts** (FAISS retrieval + ranked sources).

## Answer (from `investment_policy.txt`)

Idea-stage applicants must show **traction**: at least one of **revenue**, **growth rate**, or **active customers**. Minimum funding ask **USD 25,000**; typical seed **USD 250k–2M**. Pre-seed range **USD 250k–750k** for idea/MVP with early signals.
