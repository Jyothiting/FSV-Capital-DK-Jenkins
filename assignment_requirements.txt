TASK: Create a Startup Funding Web Form  
 
Design and develop a digital application web form for startups seeking funding from FSV 
Capital. The form should enable structured deal intake, investor-grade screening, and pipeline 
creation for DeepTech, Fintech, AI, Blockchain, and Growth-stage startups.

• Startup Name

• Year of Incorporation

 Section 4: Market Opportunity

Key Expectations 
1.  Investor-Grade Design 
• Form must reflect VC-level seriousness and credibility 
• Clean, minimal, high-trust UI (no clutter) 
• Designed for quality applications, not mass submissions 
  
2.  User Experience (Founder Side) 
• Mobile + desktop responsive 
• Completion time: 8–12 minutes max 
• Progress bar (multi-step form preferred) 
• Save & resume later option 
• Auto-validation for key fields 
  
3.  Structured Data Capture Form must capture: 
• Startup identity & founder details 
• Problem, solution, product clarity 
• Market size & opportunity 
• Traction metrics (mandatory section) 
• Funding ask & use of funds 
• Team strength & background 
• Pitch deck upload (mandatory) 
  
4.  Built-in Filtering Logic (Very Important) 
Include mandatory screening rules: 
• Revenue stage selection (Idea / MVP / Revenue / Growth) 
• Minimum traction indicators (if applicable) 
• Funding range selection 
• Sector alignment (Fintech, AI, Blockchain, etc.) 
  
5.  Investor Dashboard Ready Output Data collected must be: 
• Structured (JSON / CRM compatible) 
• Exportable to Excel / Airtable / CRM 
• Ready for deal pipeline tracking 
  
6. Document Handling & Uploading of Data  
• Pitch deck upload (PDF only) 
• Optional: demo video / product link 
• Secure file storage integration
